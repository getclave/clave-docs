You can easily sell your cryptocurrencies to receive USDC while maintaining full custody of your funds. Convert ETH, BTC, and many other tokens back to USDC on supported networks with just a few clicks.

We aggregate over multiple protocols including **Relay**, **Bungee**, **Magpie**, **LiFi**, and **ODOS** to find the best quote and route for the best possible price.

## How to Sell Tokens

To sell your tokens, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Clave app** and navigate to the **Invest** page.
2. **Select the token you want to sell** from your available balance (e.g., ETH, BTC, etc.).
3. **View token details** - tap on the token to open its details page.
4. **Press the "Sell" button** to begin the selling process.
5. **Enter the amount** you wish to sell. You can specify either the token amount or the USD equivalent.
6. **Review the quote** - the app will show you the best available price from our aggregated protocols.
7. **Click on "Review Sale"** to proceed to the next step.
8. **Confirm the sale details** and swipe to complete the transaction.

## Important Information

- **Receive USDC:** All token sales convert to USDC in your wallet
- **Swap Fee:** 0.5% fee applies to all sales
- **Supported Networks:** Available on supported networks within the Clave ecosystem
- **Best Pricing:** We aggregate multiple protocols to ensure you get the best available quote
