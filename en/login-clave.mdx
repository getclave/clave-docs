If you already have an account, you can follow these steps to log in to Clave.


### Steps to Sign In

1. **Open the Clave App** and you will **Get Started** option on the welcome screen.

2. Tap **Sign in to my account** button to proceed to the sign-in options.

3. **Choose Your Wallet**: You will be presented with a list of available wallets associated with your device. Select the wallet you want to use by tapping on it.

    <Note>
    The passkey pop-up UI might look different in various devices and operating systems.
    </Note>

4. **Authenticate with Biometric Authentication** to complete the sign-in process.

![Account Settings](/images/login-logout/login.png)

Once authenticated, you will be signed in to your Clave account, ready to go to the onchain world!
