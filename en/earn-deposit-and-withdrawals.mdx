At Clave, we have integrated Lending Pools via **Morpho** and **Aave** protocols to enable users to earn passive income by staking their assets in these pools.

To earn money on Earn Hub, you need a funded wallet first. If you haven't deposited funds into Clave, follow this [guide](./how-to-fund-your-wallet).

With Account Abstraction, users no longer need to perform multiple transactions while using DeFi applications. This is why earning on Clave is just one click away!

**Step-by-Step Guide to Start Earning:**

1. **Go to Earn** from navigation bar.

2. **View Yield Opportunities**: You'll see various yield opportunities; simply select the asset you want to deposit and continue.

3. **Select Deposit Amount**: Choose how much you want to deposit into the Earn Pool and continue.

4. **Complete biometric authentication** after reviewing the transaction.

That’s all! You’ve started to make money with your earnings.

![Earn Deposit](/images/earn/deposit.png)

### How to Withdraw Your Funds

You can withdraw your assets anytime. There's no lock-up period, and users are free to withdraw their assets whenever they want.

1. **Go to Earn** and select the position you want to withdraw from.

2. **Click the Withdraw** button.

3. **Enter the Amount You Want to Withdraw**.

4. **Click Withdraw**, and your funds will be withdrawn.

You’re done! Now you’ve withdrawn your funds and can use them anywhere.

![Earn Withdrawal](/images/earn/withdraw.png)

<Tip>
You don't need to manually collect your rewards. Thanks to Clave's yield aggregator, your rewards are automatically collected and reinvested into the pool.
</Tip>
