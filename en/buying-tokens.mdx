You can easily buy various cryptocurrencies with your USDC while maintaining full custody of your funds. Purchase ETH, BTC, and many other tokens on supported networks with just a few clicks.

We aggregate over multiple protocols including **Relay**, **Bungee**, **Magpie**, **LiFi**, and **ODOS** to find the best quote and route for the best possible price.

## How to Buy Tokens

To buy tokens, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Clave app** and navigate to the **Invest** page.
2. **Select the token you want to buy** from the available options (e.g., ETH, BTC, etc.).
3. **View token details** - tap on the token to open its details page.
4. **Press the "Buy" button** to begin the purchase process.
5. **Enter the amount** you wish to purchase in USD. The minimum buy amount is **$0.50**.
6. **Review the quote** - the app will show you the best available price from our aggregated protocols.
7. **Click on "Review Purchase"** to proceed to the next step.
8. **Confirm the purchase details** and swipe to complete the transaction.

## Important Information

- **Minimum Purchase:** $0.50 minimum buy amount
- **Payment Method:** You can buy tokens with your USDC balance
- **Swap Fee:** 0.5% fee applies to all purchases
- **Supported Networks:** Available on supported networks within the Clave ecosystem
- **Best Pricing:** We aggregate multiple protocols to ensure you get the best available quote
