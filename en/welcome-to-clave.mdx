Clave is a smart wallet that makes crypto actually usable. Built on Arbitrum and Base networks, it removes the complexity that makes crypto frustrating for most people.

## What makes Clave different

**Multichain support**:
Your wallet works across Arbitrum and Base. See all your tokens in one place, move between networks easily, and recover your account from either network.

**Automatic earnings**:
Your money can grow automatically through DeFi protocols. We find good earning opportunities so you don't have to research them yourself.

**Simple recovery**:
Lost your phone? Recover your wallet with your email or ask friends you've set as backup contacts. No need to remember complicated seed phrases.

**Easy transactions**:
- Send crypto with just a link - no copying wallet addresses
- Sometimes get free transactions when you're low on gas
- Use your fingerprint or face to approve payments
- Spend crypto with a debit card (coming soon)
