If you haven't deposited any funds to Clave, no problem! Here is the guide to depositing from CEXs and other wallets.

## Getting Your Wallet Address

To receive funds in your Clave wallet, you'll need to share your wallet address with the sender. Here's how to access it:

1. **Navigate to your deposit screen**: From your wallet's main screen, tap on "Deposit" to navigate to the wallet address section.

2. **View your address**: You'll see your wallet address displayed along with a QR code for easy sharing.

3. **Copy your address**: Use the "Copy Address" to copy your wallet address to your clipboard for sharing.

4. **Check supported networks**: Make sure to verify the supported networks. As shown in the app, you can receive funds on:
   - **Arbitrum One**
   - **Base**
   - or other supported networks as indicated in the app

5. Or, tap the "Show QR Code" button to display a scannable QR code that others can use to send you funds.


![Wallet Address Interface](/images/deposit/1.png)

**Important Notes:**
- Only send funds from supported networks shown in your app
- Double-check the network before sending funds to avoid losing them
- Your wallet address remains the same across supported networks
